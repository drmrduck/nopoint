## Notes on accuracy

- The 2009 deck's projections were aggressive and famously hand-wavey. They
    closed the round anyway. Resist the temptation to "correct" the numbers —
    quoting them faithfully is the point.
- The original used the Airbnb pre-rebrand wordmark "AirBed&Breakfast" and the
    domain `airbedandbreakfast.com`. Both appear on the deck.
- The 2009 founding team was Brian Chesky, Joe Gebbia, and Nathan Blecharczyk
    in the roles shown on the team slide. Subsequent role changes are not
    reflected here.
