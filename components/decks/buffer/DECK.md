# Buffer — 2013 seed deck (faithful recreation)

A tribute recreation of Joel Gascoigne's Buffer seed deck (October 2013), the
deck that famously raised $500K in seven days. Reproduced as a teaching example
of a metrics-led, traction-first pitch.

## Source material

The deck circulated publicly via Joel Gascoigne's blog and is republished by
Slidebean, Pitch.com, and BasedVC. All figures are quoted from the original
2013 deck — they reflect Buffer's state in late 2013, not present-day numbers.

## Design rules

- **White surface, navy ink, Buffer blue accent.** The 2013 deck used a clean
    white background with a sky-blue accent. We use bg `#FFFFFF`, text
    `#1F2A37`, accent `#168EEA` (Buffer's 2013-era blue). Soft `#F4F8FB`
    surfaces for cards. No gradients, no shadows beyond a single soft elevation.
- **Traction goes second.** The defining choice of this deck: don't waste the
    first slide on a vision statement when you have growth charts. Slide 2 is
    revenue + users + MoM growth, before any storytelling.
- **Numbers in the headline.** Almost every slide leads with a single number
    rendered at hero size (10% MoM, 800K users, $150K ARR).
- **Hypothesis language.** Buffer was an early proponent of Lean Startup
    rhetoric. The roadmap slide is framed as "experiments to run," not a
    feature list.

## Slide plan

1. Cover — name + one-line description
2. Traction — the headline metrics
3. Vision — where we're going
4. Market — why social-media-management is huge
5. Product — what we ship today
6. Business Model — freemium + paid tiers
7. Why We Will Win — defensibility
8. Roadmap — the next experiments
9. Team — three founders, two engineers, one designer
10. Investors & Press — social proof
11. Financial Projections — three-year revenue ramp
12. The Ask — $500K at $4M cap

## What we did NOT change

- The numbers (800K users, $150K ARR, 10% MoM, $400K ask). They are the deck.
- The slide order — traction-second is the lesson here.
- The "we're already growing" framing of every slide.
