## Notes

- Buffer was already profitable when raising — the deck explicitly leads with
    that fact. Resist the temptation to lead with vision; the lesson is to put
    your strongest signal first.
- Joel Gascoigne, Leo Widrich, and the early team are listed under their 2013
    titles. Subsequent role changes (Joel transitioning, etc.) are not
    reflected.
- The "$500K in 7 days" headline is real; the deck closed on those terms.
